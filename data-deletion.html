V73 Data Deletion

How to request deletion of installation-related app data.

V73 currently uses a private installation identity instead of a username-and-password account. You may request deletion of app-related backend data associated with your V73 installation.

Do not send sensitive trading credentials.

Never include a brokerage password, exchange password, API key, recovery code, seed phrase, bank credential, or other trading-platform login information in a deletion request.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email support@v73technologies.com.
  2. Use the subject line: V73 Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the private V73 installation identifier only when the app displays it for support or deletion purposes.
  4. Include your device type and a brief description that helps identify the installation data.
  5. State clearly that you want the installation-related V73 backend data deleted.

Important Before Uninstalling or Clearing App Storage

Uninstalling V73 or clearing app storage may remove the locally stored installation credential before the backend data can be identified. When practical, submit the deletion request before uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on what exists for the installation, deletion may include installation-scoped portfolio/session information, push notification identifiers, settings, confirmation or skip history, alert state, diagnostics, and related backend records.

What May Be Retained

Limited records may be retained when reasonably required for security, legal obligations, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, backup integrity, system reliability, or other legitimate business purposes.

Effect on App Functionality

Deleting installation-related backend data may remove alerts, saved settings, portfolio/session history, diagnostics, and other app state. A future app installation may be treated as a new installation.
