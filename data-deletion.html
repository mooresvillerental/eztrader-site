V73 Data Deletion

How to request deletion of app-related data.

You may request deletion of app-related data associated with V73 by emailing V73 Technologies LLC.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email support@geteztrader.com.
  2. Use the subject line: V73 Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or identifier you used with V73, if applicable.
  4. State that you want app-related V73 data deleted.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on what data exists for your app usage, deletion may include app/session information, push notification identifiers, support request data, saved app state, or related backend records.

What May Be Retained

Some limited records may be retained if required for security, legal, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, backup integrity, or other legitimate business purposes.

Important App Functionality Note

Deleting backend data may affect app functionality, alerts, saved settings, portfolio/session history, diagnostics, and support history.

Privacy Policy

For more information, review the V73 Privacy Policy.
