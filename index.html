What V73 does

A trading companion, not a broker

V73 is designed to help users review market information and keep a private, installation-scoped record of supported app activity while the user remains responsible for every brokerage or exchange action.

Setup monitoring

V73 watches supported stock and crypto setups and presents actionable user-facing states and context without publishing internal engine scores.

Portfolio tools

Users can review tracked holdings and record trades they completed on their own trading platform. A V73 record does not submit or verify a brokerage order.

Alerts and guidance

V73 can surface supported alerts and educational explanations while keeping the final decision with the user.

Safety first

Important boundaries

No automatic real trades

V73 is not a broker, exchange, custodian, bank, or investment adviser. Users make their own decisions and carry out any real trade through their own trading platform.

Keep credentials private

V73 support will never ask for a brokerage password, exchange password, API key, seed phrase, recovery code, raw V73 installation credential, or push-notification token.
