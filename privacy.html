V73 Privacy Policy

Updated: August 2, 2026

V73 is an AI-assisted market setup app operated by V73 Technologies LLC. The app is designed to help users monitor market setups, portfolio/session information, signal status, diagnostics, educational guidance, and alerts.

This Privacy Policy explains what information V73 may collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Private Installation Identity

V73 currently operates without a username-and-password account. The app uses a private installation identity issued for the app installation. The backend stores a one-way installation key and installation-scoped records so supported portfolio, history, alert, setting, and diagnostic data can remain separated from other installations.

The raw installation credential is intended for app-to-server authentication and should not be shared publicly. It is not a brokerage credential and does not provide access to a bank, broker, exchange, or trading account.

App Functionality Data

The app may request, send, store, and display portfolio/session values entered or confirmed by the user, market setup data, signal status, trade-review history, app settings, watch-only evaluation information, and diagnostic information through the V73 backend.

Notification Data

V73 uses push notifications to deliver market setup alerts and related app notifications. To support this feature, the app may collect and send an Expo push notification token or similar device notification identifier to the V73 backend.

User Actions

When a user confirms, skips, clears, resets, or updates app/session information, those actions may be sent to the V73 backend so the app can function properly and prevent duplicate confirmations.

Local App Storage

V73 may store installation identity, alert/session information, settings, and related app state locally on the device so the app can preserve its state when closed, reopened, or updated.

Support Requests

If you contact V73 for support, we may process the information you provide, such as your reply email address, device information, screenshots, error details, and the contents of your request.

2. How We Use Information

V73 uses collected information for app functionality, support, security, evaluation, and reliability, including:

  • Keeping installation-specific app data separated
  • Displaying portfolio/session information
  • Showing market setup and signal status
  • Delivering push notifications and alerts
  • Saving alert and app state
  • Recording user-confirmed or skipped setup actions
  • Preventing duplicate confirmations
  • Improving app reliability, diagnostics, and watch-only evaluation
  • Responding to support or data deletion requests

3. Brokerage and Financial Credentials

V73 does not require users to provide brokerage passwords, exchange passwords, bank credentials, API keys, seed phrases, recovery codes, or similar login credentials. Do not send these items to V73 support.

V73 does not hold customer funds and does not automatically place trades.

4. What We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos, except screenshots a user voluntarily sends for support
  • Audio recordings
  • Calendar data
  • Health data
  • Browsing history
  • Brokerage or exchange login credentials

5. Advertising and Tracking

V73 does not use collected information for third-party advertising or ad targeting.

6. Data Sharing

V73 does not sell user data.

Data may be processed through service providers necessary for app functionality, security, support, hosting, app builds, push notifications, and related infrastructure. V73 may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or V73 Technologies LLC.

7. Data Security

V73 uses HTTPS when communicating with backend services and uses installation-scoped storage to separate supported app data. While reasonable steps are taken to protect data, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

V73 keeps app functionality data only as long as reasonably needed to operate, support, secure, evaluate, and improve the app, unless a longer retention period is required by law or legitimate business needs.

You may request deletion of installation-related app data by visiting the V73 Data Deletion page or emailing support@geteztrader.com. Deleting backend data may affect alerts, settings, portfolio/session history, diagnostics, and other app functionality.

9. Trading Disclaimer

V73 provides informational market setup alerts, educational material, and app-based monitoring tools. V73 does not guarantee profits, does not provide financial advice, and does not place trades automatically. Trading involves risk, and users remain responsible for their own decisions.

10. Age Requirement

V73 is intended for users age 18 and older. It is not intended for children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised date.
