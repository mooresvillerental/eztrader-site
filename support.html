V73 Support

Support for the V73 AI-assisted market setup app.

For app support, privacy questions, installation-data deletion requests, or Google Play questions, contact V73 Technologies LLC at:

Email: support@v73technologies.com

Website: v73technologies.com

Protect your account and installation information.

V73 support will never ask you to send a brokerage password, exchange password, API key, recovery code, seed phrase, raw V73 installation credential, Expo push token, or other trading-platform login credential.

What to Include

To help us respond faster, include:

  • Your preferred reply email address
  • Your device type and Android version, when relevant
  • A brief description of the issue or request
  • Screenshots, exact error messages, and approximate dates or times when available
  • The Installation Support Code shown in V73 Settings, when support needs to identify one specific installation

Separate App Installations

V73 currently operates without a username-and-password account. Each copy of the app may receive its own private installation identity, including a regular installation and a copy installed in Secure Folder, a work profile, or another device profile.

Separate installations may have separate tracked portfolios, histories, settings, decision records, and notification registrations. Updating one copy does not merge those records with another copy.

Before Uninstalling or Clearing Storage

Do not uninstall V73 or clear its storage while a support or deletion request is being investigated unless V73 support specifically directs you to do so. Clearing storage can remove the local installation credential and Installation Support Code used to locate installation-specific backend records.

Trading and Setup Questions

V73 provides informational market setup alerts and monitoring tools. V73 does not guarantee profits, does not provide financial advice, does not hold customer funds, and does not automatically place trades. Users remain responsible for their own trading decisions and for actions taken through their own trading platform.

Age Requirement

V73 is intended for users age 18 and older who can independently understand and accept trading risk.
