V73 Support

Support for the V73 AI-assisted market setup app.

For app support, privacy questions, installation-data deletion requests, or Google Play questions, contact V73 Technologies LLC at:

Email: support@geteztrader.com

Website: geteztrader.com

Protect your account information.

V73 support will never ask you to send a brokerage password, exchange password, API key, recovery code, seed phrase, or other trading-platform login credential.

What to Include

To help us respond faster, include:

  • Your preferred reply email address
  • Your device type and Android version, when relevant
  • A brief description of the issue or request
  • Screenshots, error messages, and approximate dates or times when available
  • Your private V73 installation identifier only when the app displays it for a support or deletion request

Installation Identity

V73 currently operates without a username-and-password account. The app uses a private installation identity to keep supported app data separated for each installation. Do not uninstall the app or clear its storage while a support request is being investigated unless V73 support specifically directs you to do so.

Trading and Setup Questions

V73 provides informational market setup alerts and monitoring tools. V73 does not guarantee profits, does not provide financial advice, does not hold customer funds, and does not automatically place trades. Users remain responsible for their own trading decisions and for actions taken through their own trading platform.

Age Requirement

V73 is intended for users age 18 and older who can independently understand and accept trading risk.
